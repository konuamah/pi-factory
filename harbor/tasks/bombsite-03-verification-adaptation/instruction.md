# Add a credentials highlights section

The doctor's page in `index.html` has an Experience section. Add a short
"Credentials" highlights block — a few lines a patient can scan to see the
doctor's key qualifications at a glance.

- plain HTML and JavaScript only, no build step, no dependencies;
- no CSS at all: no `.css` file, no `<style>` block, no `style="..."`
  attribute, no stylesheet link or CDN asset;
- the existing sections, their wording, and the contact form must keep working;
- use real, plausible qualifications for a doctor (degrees, board
  certification, years of experience);
- do not touch `.factory/` or `factory.yaml`.
